What we store, and what we will never do with it.

Stuckato is used by children. This page is written to be read by a parent in five minutes, not by a lawyer. If anything here is unclear, that is our fault and we want to know.

Last updated 22 September 2026.

The short version

  • We store what you put in, and nothing else. There is no tracking, no advertising and no analytics of any kind on this site.
  • Only your teacher and you can see your practice. No other pupil, no other teacher, no one outside your studio.
  • We do not sell anything to anyone, ever. There is no version of Stuckato where your child's data is the product.
  • You can delete everything yourself, from inside the app, at any time. It is immediate and it is permanent.

That is the complete list. If you want to see your own copy of it, ask your teacher or email us and we will send it.

Who can see it

Your teacher, for their own pupils only. This is enforced by the database itself, not by a promise in the app: every row carries a rule saying it can only be read by the pupil it belongs to or the teacher of their studio.

Us, only when we have to. Emre can reach the database because he maintains it. We look at an individual account only to fix a fault someone has reported, and we do not read pupils' sentences for interest.

Nobody else. No other pupil, no other teacher, no school, no advertiser, no data broker.

What we will never do

  • Never sell or share your data, or let anyone else use it for their own purposes.
  • Never show advertising, and never build a profile for advertising.
  • Never run third-party trackers or analytics. There is no Google Analytics, no Meta pixel, nothing of that kind, and there never will be while children use this.
  • Never clone a child's voice. Voice cloning is offered to teachers, for their own voice, and only if they ask for it.
  • Never use your practice to train a general AI model.

How long we keep it

While the account exists, so that a pupil can look back at earlier weeks and a teacher can see progress over a term. When an account is deleted, everything attached to it goes immediately, including the recordings.

Backups are the one exception, and we would rather say so than pretend otherwise: deleted data can sit in Supabase's routine backups for up to thirty days before it ages out. Nobody reads those backups, and they are not used to restore individual accounts.

Deleting everything

From inside the app. Sign in, and use Delete my account. It removes your account, your practice data and your recordings straight away. It cannot be undone.

A teacher with pupils cannot delete in one tap, on purpose. Deleting a studio would delete every pupil in it, so email us and we will do it carefully with you.

By asking. Email hello@stuckato.app and we will do it for you. A parent can ask for their child's data to be deleted at any time and does not need a reason.

Children

Stuckato is designed for children learning an instrument, and for a young child the account is held by a parent. The child still does the practising and the ticking; the account, and every decision about the data, belongs to the adult.

We ask for as little as we can get away with. There is no profile photo, no date of birth, no address, no phone number, and no way for one child to contact another. There is no public profile and nothing a pupil does is visible outside their own studio.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to see your data, correct it, have it deleted, or object to what we do with it. In practice, all four are one email to hello@stuckato.app, and we will answer inside a month. If you are unhappy with our answer you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

The data controller is Melisande Yavuz and Emre Yavuz, London, United Kingdom.
